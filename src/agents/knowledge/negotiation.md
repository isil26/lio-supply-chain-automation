# Chaser and negotiation

## Escalation ladder
- R0 probe: ask for physically finished quantity, lot numbers and earliest pickup. Human approves send.
- R1 document: if no verifiable reply within 3 hours, ask for packing list, booking number or forwarder release.
- R2 incentive: at 30 hours before launch, propose an allowed lever with a response deadline.
- R3 voice: flag a human to call and prepare a cited call script.
- R4 Plan B: start priced alternatives in parallel and require PP-07 approval.

Ask for verifiable commitments: named confirmer, exact quantity and date, lot/pallet label, packing list, carrier booking, forwarder release or truck slot.
Treat date-only or “we will do our best” replies as weak evidence.
Start Plan B in parallel from R1; never wait for R4 to model alternatives.

## Archetype tactics
- Karton Nowak: art-book/sleeve/poster single source; request pallet labels and truck slots; avoid arguing capacity clauses that do not fit the express offer.
- Boise: EXW and no useful penalty leverage; ask for dock-held lot release and forwarder contact.
- Hwaseong: use contract history and renewal timing; discounted ETA and reply evidence update p(on-time).
- Pune: internal PPAP/release constraint; escalate rather than chase a supplier release that the agent cannot grant.
- Dongguan: no credible case-material walkaway; use cooperative levers rather than threats.

Never reveal deadline slack, alternative suppliers, maximum premium or missing-copy cost.
Every premium or penalty claim must come from a tool or cited contract; the agent never invents a binding term.
