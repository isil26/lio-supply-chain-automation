This pack states stable case facts. Use tools for every computed quantity, probability, ETA, cost and recommendation.

## Launch and editions
- Grand Heist 6 goes on sale Sunday 22 November 2026 at 20:00 Europe/Berlin (DC-HAM), and packed means at the distribution center.
- Two finished goods: FG-GH6-STD Standard Edition and FG-GH6-CE Collector's Edition.
- Standard BOM: CMP-GAM-001 game card, CMP-GAM-003 clear case, CMP-GAM-004 cover sleeve, CMP-GAM-011 activation-code leaflet.
- Collector BOM: CMP-GAM-001 game card, CMP-GAM-006 steel case, CMP-GAM-007 rigid magnetic-lid box, CMP-GAM-008 art book, CMP-GAM-009 folded city-map poster, CMP-GAM-010 statue, CMP-GAM-011 leaflet, plus CMP-MAG-005 lid magnets at four units per collector box.
- DC-HAM is the destination and packing site.
- The 350k claimed cases at CTA must be collected separately from the ERP stock balance; shipment and stock status are not the same field.

## Supplier and evidence concepts
- ERP stock is a booking value, not automatically usable stock.
- Usable stock excludes rejected, held or incorrectly booked batches.
- Shipment status, warehouse receipt and supplier promise are separate evidence.
- No computed numbers belong in this file. Readiness, gaps and costs come from tools and cite their outputs.
